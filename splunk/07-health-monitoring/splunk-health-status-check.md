# Splunk Health Status Check

## Objective

Review Splunk health monitoring functionality and verify operational status indicators within the Splunk Enterprise environment.

---

## Health Status Overview

Splunk provides built-in health monitoring features that display the operational status of Splunk services and system components.

Health monitoring helps identify:
- service degradation
- indexing issues
- search performance problems
- resource utilization concerns
- operational failures

---

## Accessing Splunk Health Status

While connected to Splunk Enterprise, accessed the health monitoring interface near the Administrator menu.
